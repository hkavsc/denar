Finance – Privacy policy

Finance is a small personal web app (installable PWA) used by a household of two people to record and view their own income, expenses, savings and loans. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data the app accesses

  • Google Sheets (scope spreadsheets): the app reads and writes one spreadsheet that the user chooses by entering its link. It stores the entries (date, type, category, amount, note, account, person) only in that spreadsheet.
  • Basic profile (scope userinfo.profile): the user's first name and Google account ID, used only to label entries and to pre-select the account when signing in.

How the data is used and stored

  • All data stays in the user's own Google spreadsheet and in the browser storage (localStorage) of the user's own device. The access token, name and settings are stored only on that device.
  • The app has no server of its own. No data is sent to the developer or to any third party. There is no advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • Google user data is not shared, sold or transferred, and is used only to provide the features described above.

Revoking access and deleting data

Users can sign out inside the app (Settings → Sign out), which revokes the access token. Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. All data can be deleted by deleting the spreadsheet or its rows.

Contact

Use the support email address shown on the app's Google consent screen.

Finance – Zasebnost (slovensko)

Finance je majhna osebna spletna aplikacija za gospodinjstvo dveh oseb, namenjena beleženju lastnih prihodkov, odhodkov, prihrankov in kreditov. Ni namenjena javnosti.

Do katerih Googlovih podatkov dostopa: do ene Google Preglednice, ki jo uporabnik izbere z vnosom povezave (branje in pisanje vnosov), ter do imena in ID-ja Google računa za označevanje vnosov.

Kje so podatki: samo v uporabnikovi preglednici in v pomnilniku brskalnika na njegovi napravi. Aplikacija nima strežnika, podatkov ne pošilja razvijalcu ali tretjim osebam, ne vsebuje oglasov ali sledenja in podatkov ne deli ali prodaja.

Preklic dostopa in brisanje: v aplikaciji (Nastavitve → Odjava) ali na myaccount.google.com/permissions. Podatke izbrišeš z brisanjem preglednice ali njenih vrstic.

Kontakt: e-naslov za podporo, naveden na Googlovem zaslonu za soglasje.
